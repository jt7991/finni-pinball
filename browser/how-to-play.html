How to Play

Finni the fox is throwing his first neighborhood garden party, and there’s a lot to finish before the guests arrive. The presents need wrapping, the cookies need baking, and Finni’s already getting carried away testing the disco decks. Lend a paw, rally his friends, and get the garden ready for Party Time.

Quick start

  1. Choose 1–4 players and press Play. Hold and release Launch to send the ball. Use the flippers to move the flashing top lane before the ball rolls through for a skill shot.
  2. Shoot the lit scoop on the left. Use the flippers to browse the party tasks on the display. Press Launch, Start, or Action to choose one.
  3. Follow the flashing shots. Finish the task, then shoot the scoop for a completion jackpot. Hit the four left targets to light another task.

With the default keys, use Left Shift and Right Shift to flip, hold and release Enter to launch, and press Space for Action. On a touch screen, use the sides of the playfield for the flippers and the Launch and Action buttons below it.

With a standard gamepad, use the shoulder buttons or triggers to flip. Hold and release A (Cross) to launch, press X (Square) for Action, and press Start (Options) to begin. D-pad left, up, and right nudge the table.

Party tasks

Choose every task at the lit scoop. The first three are available right away. Finish three tasks to unlock the other preparations, then finish all six to unlock Party Time.

Wrap the Presents

Available from the start. Hit the stand-up targets first, then follow the flashing shots to finish wrapping.

Send the Invitations

Available from the start. Shoot the flashing white shots. Start with the center loop, then follow the lights as ramps join in.

Blow Up the Balloons

Available from the start. Shoot the outer loops and bounce the ball into the three balloon bumpers. Both help fill the balloons.

Put Up the Decorations

Unlocks after three tasks. Shoot the flashing green shots. The pair changes after each hit.

Bake the Cookies

Unlocks after three tasks. Shoot the flashing shot to bake the cookies and score a super jackpot. Solid blue shots score regular Cookie Jackpots.

Hang the Piñata

Unlocks after three tasks. Two extra balls join when the task starts. Shoot the flashing green shots to hoist the piñata.

Party Time

Unlocks after all six preparations. Select it at the lit scoop to start timed multiball. Follow the flashing shot as it moves around the table.

Multiballs and side modes

Cake Time Multiball

Clear the three drop targets in front of Finni. Hit the toy twice to start your first Cake Time. Later starts take up to four hits.

Hit Finni for frosting jackpots, collect the lit-shot jackpots for candles and decorations, then shoot the spinners before time runs out.

Friendship Multiball

When Lock is lit, shoot the right ramp to gather a friend. Hit the four right targets to light Lock when it’s off. The third lock starts multiball.

Collect all six lit jackpots, then shoot the right ramp for the super jackpot.

Disco Loops

Get 50 spins on the left spinner while no party task is running. Once the disco ball starts turning, chain shots through the outer loops for jackpots.

Disco Multiball

After Disco Loops ends, keep building the left-spinner count to 100 with no task running. Either ramp collects a super jackpot, and either spinner raises its value.

Supply Run Multiball

Collect 75 supplies from lit shots, then shoot the scoop to start. Clear the six lit jackpots and shoot the scoop again for the super jackpot.

Streamer Rush

Follow the flashing Ribbon lights to collect six ribbons. The sixth starts the timer. Shoot the lit lanes and ramps for Streamer Jackpots.

Spinner Rush

Get 25 spins across either spinner to start the timer. Keep spinning either one for growing points.

Confetti Burst

With no party task running, shoot the left loop, then go straight to the right ramp. The combo awards 10 Confetti and a points bonus.

Mystery

Clear both sets of four targets to light Mystery, then shoot the scoop. The game picks a surprise such as bonus supplies, points, or lighting an extra ball.
