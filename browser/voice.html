Pick a small recording set

Start with three audition lines per role: a short cue, a longer line, and an excited moment. Choose a reference take together, then keep pronunciation, volume, and energy consistent across the team. Choose another small set only if you want more to record.

Choose your role and line

Record one line per take. Leave a brief pause before and after speaking. Keep lines short. Record in a quiet room, about a hand’s width from the mic, with no music playing.

Find the Finni sound

A gentle pitch lift, rumble filter, compression, and matched volume.

Compression evens out loud and quiet words. Pitch keeps the line’s length; larger shifts can sound more synthetic.

32 kHz stereo WAV · matched speech level · peaks capped at −1 dB
