Controls

Hold either Shift key to flip. On a phone, hold the lower left or right of the table, or use the marked buttons. Both sides work together.

Hold Launch or Enter to pull the plunger; release to shoot. Press 1 to start. Space confirms a task or uses an earned special ability. Z, X and / nudge. Repeated quick nudges cause a tilt.

Performance limits pixel density and uses simple lighting. Changes apply immediately.

Flippers choose a task while the ball is in the scoop. Action confirms the task or uses an earned special ability. Start can add up to four players during the first ball.
