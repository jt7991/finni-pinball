About the game

This Three.js port uses the original table rules, artwork and audio. Its independent physics are being matched against the VPX reference. The original DMD sprites are rendered separately so messages and animations stay visible in every camera view.

JP Salas’s Deadpool v7, Finni party artwork, vpinball-wasm 0.3.4. Credits and licenses are in the project README.
