three/flipper-float-math.js adapts the float atan/atan2 arithmetic from:
https://github.com/emscripten-core/emscripten/blob/main/system/lib/libc/musl/src/math/atanf.c
https://github.com/emscripten-core/emscripten/blob/main/system/lib/libc/musl/src/math/atan2f.c

The exact reference C sources are included in references/vpx-render as
musl-atanf.c and musl-atan2f.c. This preserves native Float32 mesh-generation
rounding. It does not replace the gameplay physics math or RNG.
