context state from Wine/libwinevbs. Copyright 2011 Jacek Caban for CodeWeavers.

The JavaScript adaptation uses a DataView to preserve the native Single and
Double bit-pattern seeds, Math.imul for modulo-2^32 arithmetic, and per-script
interpreter registration. The published VPX-engine recording in
tests/fixtures/vpx-script-random.json verifies the algorithm and conversions.
